Restore purchases

Pro is unlocked through Apple. To restore it on a new device, or after reinstalling:

  1. Open PhotoSweep.
  2. Go to Settings, then Get PhotoSweep Pro.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases and sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro.

Your purchase status is checked with Apple each time the app opens. If restore does not work, check that you are signed in with the correct Apple ID in iOS Settings, then try again.

Cancel a subscription

PhotoSweep cannot cancel a subscription for you, because the transaction is with Apple. To cancel:

  1. Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  2. Find PhotoSweep Pro and tap Cancel Subscription.
  3. Your Pro access continues until the end of the current paid period.

To request a refund, use Apple Support or reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles refunds, not PhotoSweep.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no sign in, and no personal details are requested.

Does PhotoSweep work offline?

Yes. Scanning, grouping, and review all happen on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, so no connection is needed.

Will PhotoSweep delete photos by itself?

Never. Every group needs your review and your confirmation. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days.

What is the free limit?

Free deletes up to 20 photos. Pro deletes unlimited photos and adds large-video cleanup and cleanup report export. Free scans and reviews are unlimited.

Why does it say a photo is a duplicate?

Exact duplicates have the same file contents. Similar photos are burst or near-identical shots taken seconds apart. You pick the keeper in each group; the rest are proposed for deletion.

Why is the space freed different from the estimate?

Storage figures in the app are estimates. Photos may report slightly different sizes, and space is only actually freed once Recently Deleted is emptied in Photos.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app deletes your scan results and settings stored on your device. There is no server copy to recover. Your photos stay in Photos unless you deleted them from there.

Does PhotoSweep see my photos?

Only the ones you allow. It supports limited photo access, so it only sees the photos you choose to share. It reads a downscaled copy of each image to compare it with your other photos on-device.
