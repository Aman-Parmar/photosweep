# PhotoSweep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

## Short version

PhotoSweep scans your photo library on your phone. Your photos, thumbnails, and scan results never leave this device. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party service that receives your data.

## What the app accesses

- **Your photo library** (with your permission). PhotoSweep reads photo and video metadata — and a downscaled copy of each image, only to compare it with your other photos on-device — to find duplicates, similar photos, blurry photos, screenshots, and large videos. It supports limited photo access: it only sees the photos you choose to share.
- **Nothing else.** No location, no contacts, no microphone, no health data, no advertising identifier.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. There is no server, no account, and no analytics. No photo, thumbnail, hash, or scan result is transmitted anywhere, to anyone, for any purpose.

## Deletion

Nothing is deleted without you reviewing the group first and confirming. When you confirm, deleted photos and videos move to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days. After 30 days Photos deletes them permanently.

## Storage estimates

Storage figures shown in the app are estimates. Photos may report slightly different sizes, and space is only actually freed once Recently Deleted is emptied.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). PhotoSweep does not see or store your payment information. A 7-day free trial is available on the yearly subscription; Apple charges on trial end unless you cancel before day 7. Cancel anytime in iOS Settings.

## Children

PhotoSweep is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
